Why we are called Auren

The name comes from the Latin Aurum, which means gold. I wanted a name that suggests purity and value without announcing it.

My grandfather ran a small cloth shop, and he used to say a good fabric never needs a sales pitch. In 2026 I opened Auren on that idea: fewer pieces, better cloth, no loud logos.

Come in on WhatsApp, tell me what you need it for, and I will tell you honestly whether we have the right piece.

Elegance is never loud.

  • Muted champagne-gold detailing, never shiny or flashy
  • Structured silhouettes that hold shape after many washes and wears
  • Fabric checked by hand before every batch is accepted
  • No fast trends. The same core pieces, refined each season
  • Fit advice by chat before you pay, so there are fewer returns
